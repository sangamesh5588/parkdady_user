Privacy Policy for QuickPark

Last Updated: December 30, 2025

Effective Date: December 30, 2025

Important: This privacy policy describes how QuickPark ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application. By using QuickPark, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you create an account or use our services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number
  • Profile Information: User role (renter or host), profile preferences
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle make, model, license plate number for booking purposes
  • Payment Information: Payment method details processed securely through Razorpay (we do not store full credit card numbers)

1.2 Location Information

To provide our core parking search and navigation services, we collect:

  • Precise Location Data: GPS coordinates and geolocation data to show nearby parking spaces
  • Background Location: Location data while the app is in the background during active bookings to help you navigate to your parking spot
  • Address Information: Street addresses for parking locations and booking details

1.3 Camera and QR Code Data

  • Camera Access: Used exclusively for scanning QR codes to verify parking check-in/check-out
  • QR Code Information: Booking verification codes generated for your reservations

1.4 Usage and Device Information

  • App Activity: Features you use, actions you take, booking history, search queries
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Network Information: IP address, mobile network information

1.5 Information from Third-Party Services

  • Social Sign-In: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from these services
  • Google Maps: Map data and place information to display parking locations

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.1 Provide Our Services

  • Enable you to search for and book parking spaces
  • Process payments for your bookings
  • Show nearby parking availability using your location
  • Generate and verify QR codes for parking check-in
  • Manage your bookings and booking history
  • Send booking confirmations and notifications

2.2 Improve and Personalize Services

  • Understand how users interact with our app
  • Improve search algorithms and recommendations
  • Personalize your experience based on preferences
  • Develop new features and services

2.3 Communication

  • Send you booking confirmations and updates
  • Notify you about parking availability
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Send important service announcements

2.4 Safety and Security

  • Verify your identity and prevent fraud
  • Protect against unauthorized access
  • Ensure compliance with our terms of service
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our policies

2.5 Legal Compliance

  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Respond to legal requests and prevent harm
  • Exercise or defend legal claims

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following situations:

3.1 With Parking Space Hosts

When you make a booking, we share necessary information with the parking space host, including:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Vehicle details
  • Booking dates and times

3.2 Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business:

  • Supabase: Cloud database and backend services (hosting in secure data centers)
  • Razorpay: Payment processing (PCI DSS compliant)
  • Google Maps Platform: Map display and place search
  • Google/Apple: For authentication if you choose to sign in with these services

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

  • Legal processes (subpoenas, court orders)
  • Government requests
  • Protection of our rights and safety
  • Prevention of fraud or security threats

3.4 Business Transfers

If QuickPark is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure Storage: Data is stored in secure, encrypted databases managed by Supabase
  • Payment Security: Payment information is processed by PCI DSS compliant Razorpay; we do not store full card details
  • Access Controls: Row-level security policies ensure users can only access their own data
  • Regular Security Audits: We regularly review and update our security practices

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Update

You can access and update your account information at any time through the app's Profile section.

5.2 Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data. To delete your account:

  • Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account in the app, or
  • Contact us at support@quickpark.app

Please note: Some information may be retained for legal or legitimate business purposes (e.g., transaction records for accounting).

5.3 Location Permissions

You can control location permissions through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Apps > QuickPark > Permissions > Location

Note: Denying location access will limit core functionality like finding nearby parking.

5.4 Notification Preferences

You can manage notification settings in the app or through your device settings.

5.5 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of promotional emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any marketing email we send.

5.6 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your personal data in a portable format by contacting us.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax and accounting requirements)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Specific retention periods:

  • Account Information: Until you delete your account, plus 90 days
  • Booking Records: 7 years for financial and legal compliance
  • Payment Information: Managed by Razorpay per their retention policy
  • Location Data: Deleted after booking completion unless needed for dispute resolution

7. Children's Privacy
